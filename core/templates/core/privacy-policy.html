Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Our privacy policy ensures that all personal information collected is securely processed, stored, and used in compliance with Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) to protect your privacy and rights.

Introduction

Stalwart Business Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") operates as a staffing agency in Malaysia and is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia.

Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following types of personal data:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, contact details (email, phone), address, date of birth, NRIC/Passport number.
  • Employment Information: Work experience, skills, qualifications, job preferences, salary expectations.
  • Financial Information: Bank account details for payroll and other financial transactions.
  • Other Information: Any additional details provided in your resume, cover letter, or during interviews.

How We Collect Information

We collect personal data through various methods:

  • Directly from candidates during registration, resume submission, and interviews.
  • From third parties such as former employers, referees, or background checks (with your consent).
  • Automatically through our website when you browse or submit forms.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal data to:

  • Facilitate recruitment, staffing, and placement services.
  • Match job seekers with potential employers.
  • Communicate job opportunities and application updates.
  • Process payroll and manage employee benefits (for those hired through our agency).
  • Comply with legal requirements and regulations.

Disclosure of Personal Data

We may share your personal data with:

  • Prospective employers to facilitate job placements.
  • Third-party service providers, such as background check agencies or payroll processors.
  • Legal authorities if required by law or in compliance with regulatory requirements.

Data Retention

We will retain your personal data for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to comply with legal and regulatory obligations. After this period, your data will be securely deleted.

Your Rights

Under the PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access and correct your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.
  • Object to the use of your data for marketing purposes.

You may contact us at hello@stalwart.asia to exercise these rights.

Security of Your Information

We implement security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and you are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at: hello@stalwart.asia.
